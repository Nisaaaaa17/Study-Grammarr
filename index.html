To Be

Know the usage of am, is, and are.

What is To Be?

To be is used to connect the subject with information about the subject.

Usage of To Be

Subject To Be
I am
He is
She is
It is
You are
We are
They are

Example Sentences

  • I am a student.
  • She is happy.
  • They are students.
  • We are friends.
  • He is a teacher.

💡 Remember!

I → am
He / She / It → is
You / We / They → are
